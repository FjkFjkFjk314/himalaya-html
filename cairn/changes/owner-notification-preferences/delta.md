## ADDED Requirements

Owner chat shall list active notification rules and stage additions and removals. Applying a change requires explicit confirmation through the owner interface. The preview shall identify the matching criteria and delivery choice. The assistant shall communicate naturally in Chinese and accommodate explicitly saved style preferences.

## MODIFIED Requirements

Eligible new mail shall use the newest matching approved rule to select ordinary, time-sensitive, digest or silent delivery. A delivery preference shall preserve classification and important-mail memory. Operational alerts shall retain their independent delivery policy.
